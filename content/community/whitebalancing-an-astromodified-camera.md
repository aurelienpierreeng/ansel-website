Hello,

Im very new to Ansel as I literally found out about the software yesterday. I am however very intrigued and will likely migrate soon. So my one camera has to do for all my photography interests. Recently I decided to astromodify my Nikon which means I opened it up and removed a filter infront of the sensor. In essence my camera now captures a greater spectrum of light as illustrated in the following diagram.

*\[attachment lost: image was hosted on the retired forum\]*

My question now is the following at what point do I correct this color cast? The Whitebalance Module expects the camera reference at D65, did I change that by removing the filter? I can produce similar results by changing the RGB values in Whitebalance or Colorcalibration.

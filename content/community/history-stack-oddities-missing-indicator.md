*Posted by **koslowj** on 2025-02-10.*

In version 12afe31 I just noticed that I cannot clear the history of an image, if I want to start from scratch. Moreover, in the darkroom, when I try to copy the history stack, I get a warning "Copy is allowed only with exactly one image selected", even though the image in question has no further copies. Furthermore, the little triangle indicating the active image in the filmstrip has disappeared. Sometimes the enclosing frame if this image is slightly highlighted, but not always, e.g., when the mouse pointer is between the film strip and the currently opened picture.

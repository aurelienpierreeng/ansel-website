*Posted by **Steve** on 2024-08-27.*

Open image in Darkroom/select Exposure/select Blending/select Drawn Mask/select Add Gradient/position Gradient/adjust Exposure

Now look and watch in the Exposure Module where it says 'Drawn mask: 1 shape used'. While watching, click on Compress History Stack. ':no mask used'

## Replies

**Lukas** — 2023-06-21

Your assumption is right. The modules appear in predefined groups only. They are thought to represent a workflow from left-to-right.

> Moving between workflow steps means moving between module tabs, and can be done with Ctrl+Tab, wheel scroll over tabs, or right click on tabs -\> select in menu

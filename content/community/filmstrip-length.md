*Posted by **Steve** on 2025-03-16.*

I think the FilmStrip should start at the right border of the Left Panel and end at the left border of the Right Panel. The FilmStrip is robbing real-estate from the Left & Right Panels. There really is no need to have the FilmStrip use all the screen horizontal real-estate. Left & Right Panel real-estate is VERY valuable.

Hi,

The one thing that came out of the [Darktable survey](https://eng.aurelienpierre.com/2023/01/who-are-the-darktable-users/) is that Darktable's users show an abnormal share of programmers, scientists and highly-educated people, and a surprisingly low rate of women, artists and minimally-educated people. That wouldn't be a problem if Darktable was an engineering tool, but it doesn't well represent the general population of image makers, which means they are filtered out.

There are several possible explanations to that, but it definitely has something to do with being trapped in open-source men-caves *(note : FLOSS = Free/Libre Open-Source Software)* :

1.  development and debugging happen on Github (previously on Redmine), which is deterring for non-programmers and non-English speakers,
2.  software beta-testing is possible only to those able to compile the source code,
3.  users of FLOSS are seen as ugly ducks on mainstream photo forums, which are all about commercial options,
4.  FLOSS-centric imaging forums have an annoying tendency of circling back real quick to "best distro" and "softy-software" tech discussions that don't interest people outside of the FLOSS believers spheres,
5.  FLOSS-centric forums are not much interested in actual picture editing passed the typical "+200 % sharpness", "+150 % contrast" and "+100 % saturation", with a fair share of "rule of thirds" and sweeping statements about the nature of art (as if only masterpieces were art).

When you are on the look-out for places where to find advice to get pictures edited with FLOSS, unfortunately you are left with not much.

The truth is software doesn't matter. Skilled retouchers will get good results out of even the shittiest of applications. That might take ages if the app is bad, though, but still : software is only a mean to an end, and the true limit is your skills.

In the past few months, I have come to realize that a lot of Darktable users came to Darktable because of [my videos](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCmsSn3fujI81EKEr4NLxrcg). What drove them was, for a small part, the color abilities of the software, but for the most part, it was only knowing that someone could help them get results. Those people will not be found on FLOSS-centric forums, they are on Youtube for tutorials and contact me directly by email for questions.

Unfortunately, answering the load of emails I get has been a time-consuming task for the past years. So, as I took over Darktable to produce a more rational and user-friendly Ansel, this is also an opportunity to reach the real digital photographers looking to get pictures done, no matter their OS and the amount of features and ergonomics they accept to forfeit in the name of FLOSS.

Publications can for now be written either in English or in French, the selection is made when creating new discussions or editing old ones. More languages can be added in the future if there is enough demand. I can personaly moderate discussions in English, French and German, so other languages will need moderators.

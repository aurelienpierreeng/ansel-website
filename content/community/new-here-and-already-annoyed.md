*Posted by **Magnus Berg** on 2025-04-24.*

I found Ansel yesterday, downloaded an app image, and clicked around a bit.

I sympathized with the developer's ideas. I think Darktable has become too cluttered.

I decided today to start providing financial support. I went in and paid 30 euros (5 per month). There were already TWO payment options, and I chose Librapay.

When I went to check out my Ansel Community account, I discovered that you can become a premium member there. I wish I had been informed of this a little earlier. I would obviously have chosen a premium membership for 5 euros per month rather than just donating. So now I feel cheated.

But I really hope that Ansel develops well and that I get value for the donation I have made so far, even if I don't get access to what would have been included in a premium membership.

for Circle & Eclipse (Add Path works)

Updated Method: (Seems to NOT work while in ISO Color Assessment) Open Retouch module/select Shape and place in position. Close Retouch module. Re-open Retouch module. Click ONCE on Input shape, then click on Input shape and now you can move it

  

OLD METHOD: select Retouch module/select Shape/position & adjust/Compress history stack/select Retouch module/click on the Input shape.

Input will now be applied.

From March 2023 to May 2025, Ansel ran a community forum at `community.ansel.photos`. In June
2025 it was taken offline: the site had been compromised and a counterfeit site was being served
from inside the forum software, so the whole installation had to be removed.

The discussions themselves survived, in a database dump. This section republishes the ones worth
keeping — problems reported and solved, questions about the software, user stories and feedback,
and the decisions taken along the way with the reasoning behind them. They are read-only and
unedited apart from formatting, and each page keeps its original address so old links and search
results still lead somewhere.

Most pages link to a snapshot of the original in the Internet Archive, so you can check this copy
against the forum as it stood. Six threads here exist in no database at all: they had already been
deleted from the forum before the final dump, and survive only because a crawler had captured
them.

Two things to keep in mind while reading: answers may refer to versions of Ansel that have since
changed, and **image attachments are gone** — they lived on the forum's own server, which did not
survive the shutdown.

Member introductions and forum housekeeping are not republished, but nothing was thrown away:
the complete export is kept privately, and any thread left out of this section can be restored on
request.

Current discussion happens on
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/aurelienpierreeng/ansel/discussions).

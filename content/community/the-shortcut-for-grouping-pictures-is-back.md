*Posted by **koslowj** on 2025-02-04.*

It may have happened earlier, but as of version d88ee95 I've noticed that the long missing Ctrl-g shortcut for grouping images is back! As I use this a lot for grouping HDR-images, it is a big improvement not having to move the mouse pointer all the way up to the menu section and then back again to the selected group. Thanks!
